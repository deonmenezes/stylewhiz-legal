StyleWhiz AI — Terms of Use

Last updated: July 15, 2026

1. Eligibility

StyleWhiz AI is intended only for adults. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account, use the app, or access its AI features.

2. The Service

StyleWhiz AI provides AI-powered personal styling and a virtual try-on experience. For clothing with an approved on-model catalog scene, the service may synthesize your visible appearance, build, and body proportions into that catalog outfit and composition while removing the catalog model’s identity and body. Product-only clothing references and makeup or accessory requests use your photo’s composition. Every result is AI-generated, is for inspiration, and may not perfectly represent your appearance, proportions, garment details, or real-world fit. Exported and shared generated images include a visible “AI-generated” label in the image pixels.

3. Photo Processing and AI Consent

Before AI is first enabled, the app asks for your explicit permission, confirmation that you are 18 or older, a pledge to use only photos of yourself—never another person or a minor—and acknowledgement that your visible appearance, build, and body proportions may be synthesized into an approved catalog-model composition. If you allow, consent version 3, its time, and your adult confirmation are saved to your account; version 3 identifies the disclosure and required-acknowledgement set shown in that flow. The grant applies to later AI requests until you disable AI, so permission is not technically re-confirmed for every photo. A material photo-purpose change requires renewed consent.

Google’s paid Gemini API receives only your chosen photo for photo analysis. Try-on generation and visual quality analysis also receive the selected catalog references, server-resolved composition mode, and non-personal catalog name, category, subcategory, and description; quality analysis receives the candidate result; and one bounded text list of visible defects is sent with the single retry after a failed candidate. For approved on-model clothing scenes, the quality check compares the candidate with your self-photo for visible appearance and body-proportion consistency and with the catalog reference for composition and garment fidelity. Product-only clothing references, makeup, and accessories instead use your photo’s composition. The quality check does not identify you or create or compare faceprints, body measurements, embeddings, or reusable biometric templates.

Google does not use paid Gemini API prompts, files, or responses to improve or train its general-purpose products or models. Google retains prompts, contextual information, and outputs for 55 days solely to detect and prevent prohibited use, maintain service safety and security, and make required legal or regulatory disclosures. Content flagged by safety or abuse-detection systems may be assessed by authorized Google employees. Abuse-monitoring data is not used to train or fine-tune AI/ML models other than models used specifically for policy enforcement.

4. Pricing

StyleWhiz AI includes five successful AI try-ons per day at no charge. You may optionally purchase StyleWhiz Pro, an auto-renewable monthly subscription that unlocks unlimited AI try-ons while the subscription is active. The price shown in the app is the localized price provided by Apple for your App Store storefront.

5. Subscription Billing and Cancellation

Purchases use Apple StoreKit. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. StyleWhiz Pro renews automatically each month unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current subscription period. Your Apple Account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before that period ends. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your App Store account settings. Use Restore Purchases in the app to restore an active subscription on a supported device.

Apple processes subscription payments. Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement also applies.

6. Your Privacy Choices

You can turn off future AI processing, replace or remove source photos, delete saved looks, and delete your account from Profile. Removing or replacing a source photo deletes the prior private file; for a verified pre-migration legacy photo only, this may contact AWS S3 solely to delete that file. Account deletion removes account-linked profile, styling, activity, entitlement and try-on records, private source photos, and generated-result files. Restricted UUID-keyed deletion-control records, and exceptional UUID-keyed storage-reconciliation records after an ambiguous upload, may remain without a fixed retention period. They contain no photo bytes and are not used for personalization. Account deletion does not shorten Google’s separate limited retention, remove Apple’s records, or cancel an Apple subscription.

7. Acceptable Use — Your Photo Only

StyleWhiz AI is a personal styling tool. Upload only photos of yourself. Do not upload photos of other people, photos of minors, or any unlawful or explicit imagery. Outfits come exclusively from our curated catalog, and every generated result is intended to be a fully clothed styling visualization. You may not use the service to undress, sexualize, impersonate, harass, or exploit anyone. Misuse may result in suspension or termination of access.

8. Disclaimer

The service is provided “as is.” Styling suggestions are probabilistic and should be used as a reference for your own decisions.
